Problem 01

Traces come from a single generation pass at 16,384 tokens. Because generation is autoregressive, the first N tokens of a long run are exactly what a budget‑N run would have produced, so every smaller budget here is the real thing rather than an approximation — an 8k condition derived this way reproduced a genuine 8k run to within two samples. Per‑sample forcing outcomes were measured at 8,192 and 16,384 tokens only; at other budgets the toggle is disabled. The paper reports 91.4% for this benchmark under an unconstrained budget.
